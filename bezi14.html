Peep the Animal

Published Nov 6, 2026 • Updated Nov 6, 2026

Entry to Bezi jam 14 with KINGEMU246 done in 4 days. Made in Godot and Kingemu drew all the art. I made the music and developed. You can play above or visit the itch page here: https://speedlimit35.itch.io/peep-the-animal

Hint for gameplay: (the mimicks will blink while normal animals have idle animations. Also animals will run away from mimics.)

How we made:

The theme was hard to think of anything. We were trying to think of the scariest thing we knew and then suddenly we remembered a very scary video called PEEP THE HORROR. And then we also thought we should have a simple game because we are not familiar with game engines so it converged to catching these animals. But we have to add liars so we added mimics.

Our main problem was making it fun to play. The gameplay basically boils down to the binary decision of catching an animal or cutting our catcher. So there is not much complexity to the game or much of a looping factor. So we tried a lot of ways to make it more interactive where the play must observe all the animals. For example we added a hunger meter that decreases forcing the player to play the game. We also added a time for the catcher to launch and come back so catches and wrong decisions are punished. In this way we can make the player peep the creatures. And this will hopefully lead them to finding some unsettling things about some animals... Some tips is to start early 😹 .
