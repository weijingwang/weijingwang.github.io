Team Chinkaraoshiman team entry for Pyweek 42 I did with Chis and Apex. The theme was "Borrowed Time". You can find our entry here: [https://pyweek.org/e/chinkaraoshim42/](https://pyweek.org/e/chinkaraoshim42/)

## Game Concept
Its based on the popular video of the goat jumping into the fireplace. And it is a visual novel. We only had 2 days to make it so we used whatever ideas that came out.

## Some thoughts
I drew all the art and composed all the music and did the coding part as well. The planning by Chis and writing and game design by him helped a lot and the sound effects and a featured music by Apex also helped but I think by the end we all were close to dying.

It was pretty fun and we are all proud of it. It is released on Mac and windows as an app on the Github release so you can try playing it easily: [https://github.com/weijingwang/myth-beast/releases/tag/v1.0.1](https://github.com/weijingwang/myth-beast/releases/tag/v1.0.1)

I will update when I record a playthrough video and when the results of the game jam come out.
